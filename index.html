Inverter & Charge Controller Selection

Charge Controllers: MPPT vs. PWM

Your charge controller regulates the voltage and current coming from your solar panels to the battery. The two main types are:

  • PWM (Pulse Width Modulation): A simpler, less expensive option. Best for smaller systems where panel voltage is close to battery voltage.
  • MPPT (Maximum Power Point Tracking): More advanced and efficient. It can convert excess voltage into amperage, which means you get more power from your panels. MPPT is ideal for larger systems, especially when the panel voltage is significantly higher than the battery voltage.

Hybrid Inverters

A hybrid inverter, also known as a multi-mode inverter, allows you to connect your solar panels, batteries, and the utility grid. Key features to consider:

  • Grid-Tie Capability: Can feed excess solar power back to the grid.
  • Battery Storage: Can charge and discharge a battery bank, providing backup power during outages.
  • Built-in Charge Controller: Many hybrid inverters include an integrated MPPT charge controller, simplifying your system.

Surge & Voltage Protection Guide

Surge protectors are essential for protecting your solar installation from voltage spikes. Here are some best practices:

  • AC & DC Protection: Install surge protective devices (SPDs) on both the AC and DC sides of your inverter.
  • Grounding: Ensure your system is properly grounded according to local electrical codes.
  • Placement: Place SPDs as close as possible to the equipment they are protecting.

Changeover Switch Guide

A changeover switch allows you to safely switch between grid power and your solar system. Key considerations include:

  • Manual vs. Automatic: Choose between a manual or automatic transfer switch (ATS) based on your needs and budget.
  • Sizing: The switch must be rated to handle the maximum current from both your grid connection and your solar system.
  • Installation: Always have a qualified electrician install the changeover switch to ensure safety and compliance with local regulations.
